soundfont.sf2 (TimGM6mb.sf2)
============================

The bundled General MIDI instrument bank used for MIDI playback
(soundfont.sf2, in the repository root) is "TimGM6mb.sf2", a small but
complete GM SoundFont by Tim Brechbill.

Some of the samples were created by Tim Brechbill himself; all others are
either in the public domain or fall under the terms of the GNU General
Public License. The SoundFont as a whole is distributed under the GNU
General Public License, version 2 (GPL-2).

It was originally shipped with MuseScore 1.0.

Source / mirror: https://github.com/arbruijn/TimGM6mb
